Promo · Tier 1

THE PAGE

$49 one-time

A museum-grade release page for one record: your cover, a live countdown, the Smart Link with a copy button, your own reel or video embedded — and the full release registry displayed with pride: release number, ISRC, UPC, credits.

Two editions when the art requires it: a full edition and a public-share edition. Delivered as a public link that is yours to send.

Promo · Tier 2

THE PACK

$149 one-time

Everything in The Page, plus the tools to push the record: a 30-second promo commercial cut from the master on the hook, story and post visuals, a creator tool sheet with every link and credit in one place, and a dated promo push plan with the posts written and ready.

Posting fires on your word, on the plan's dates: announce · tease · release day · follow-up.

Promo · Tier 3

THE CAMPAIGN

$299 one-time

Everything in The Pack, plus the house runs the rollout with you: the cadence executed across the house's surfaces, a Spotify Canvas loop for the track, cross-links through the Lattice so every surface points at your record, and a release-week report — what went out, where, and what it did.

For scale: a single managed playlist campaign elsewhere starts at $280 — for that one channel alone.

Promo · Tier 4 · The Full Treatment

THE FULL BRIDGE

$499 one-time

Everything in The Campaign, plus the attached set: the house strikes art and coin pieces around your record — cover-variation lanes and a coin pair — fingerprinted, sealed, and registered to the release. Error variants are kept and counted rarer, per house doctrine; corrected pieces are made for use.

This is the Mac & Cheese treatment, whole. The set is the house's creation and lives on your page with a tap-to-flip coin.

Example · The Catalog Campaign

49 songs brought home

What: The Lost Vaults, Vol. 1 — the cover of a recovered-catalog campaign. How: 49 songs from 2011–2013, lost when an era of the internet was deleted, were located, extracted, restored, transcribed, and sealed. Result: 15 releases — 8 singles and 7 volumes — rolling out on Fridays from 18 Dec 2026 to 9 Apr 2027.

Example · The Art Lanes

Every release gets its lanes

What: the Basquiat-lane variation of the Vol. 1 cover. How: each release receives variation pieces in the house lanes — Basquiat, Codex, coin — struck from the cover of record. Result: 35 sealed pieces across the seven volumes, one master fingerprint for the whole set.

Example · The Cover, Reimagined

From a 3rd-grade photograph

What: the cover of record for "AC State of Mind Freestyle." How: built from the artist's own 1986–87 class photograph — the original kept untouched, as all originals are — and carried into the Basquiat lane. Result: the founder picked it over every candidate, and it was filed with the distributor as the release's official cover.

Example · The Fifteen

Fifteen singles, one landing date

What: the conformed cover sheet for fifteen singles. How: every cover rebuilt to a single canonical standard — 3000×3000, DSP-safe — with a 30-second commercial cut for each. Result: 13 of the 15 delivered to 64 destinations for Fri 27 Nov 2026, the remaining two carried through the distributor's own desk until they land.

The results, in one line: a first outside artist crossed in a single day · 49 recovered songs across 15 scheduled releases · 15 singles conformed, commercialed, and delivered to 64 destinations · every piece fingerprinted, sealed, and registered. That is what a crossing buys.

Terms, plain

  • One-time prices. You pay for the work once; the work is yours to use forever.
  • Your music stays yours. 100% of copyrights, 100% of royalties — these packs never touch either.
  • The attached set (Full Bridge / à la carte) is the house's creation; its sales and licensing are the house's, and any kickback to the artist is the founder's gift on his timing — never a term owed.
  • Turnaround: Page 1–2 days · Pack 2–3 days · Campaign across the release window · Full Bridge 3–5 days plus the campaign window.
  • Distribution itself rides the house's three distribution packages — a promo pack can ride on any of them, or stand alone for a record distributed elsewhere.

We can sign the whole world. The bridge has no border: every artist, every country, every catalog — the crossing is the same. Your record stays yours; the house builds the layer around it; the whole world hears it.
