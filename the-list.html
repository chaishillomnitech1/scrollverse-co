Why an owned room

No platform exports its followers. Consolidation therefore works the only honest way it can: every surface invites its people to walk through the same door and give an email voluntarily. No scraping. No bought lists. No harvested addresses.

  • The rail is a tool; the names are the house's. The list stays exportable, so it can move if a service ever closes.
  • Every surface becomes a door. Sites, artifacts, visuals, music pages, storybooks, podcast descriptions, certificates, and QR twins all carry the same invitation.
  • Spotify is the streaming home base. The second tap is a follow for Chais the great on Spotify, so email and Release Radar can reach the same fan twice.
  • No data is sold or shared. The List is for ScrollVerse releases, drops, openings, and news — signal, not spam.
