How it works

  • 1 · The fit call. A working conversation, not a pitch: what your business does, what's breaking, what you wish existed. If we can't fit it, we say so on the call.
  • 2 · Stack design. A written design — family, tier, build order, the set price, and what you own at the end. Yours to keep either way.
  • 3 · The build. In stages, with working pieces shown as they land. Never a black box.
  • 4 · Handover. Source, data, documentation, walkthrough. You hold everything.
  • 5 · Maintenance (optional). By separate agreement, we maintain and extend the stack. Optional means optional — the handover is complete without it.
