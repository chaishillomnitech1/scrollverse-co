Place Yours — the open door

The Launchpad was not built for one house. What the founder wants for himself, he wants for all — so the same door he walked through stands open to every creator.

Here is how a placement works:

  • Bring your creation to the house — reach the house through its open doors and present the work: what it is, that it is yours, and where it lives.
  • Reviewed by the house — every placement is read and checked by the house before it stands here. The standard is the house's standard: real work, truly yours, nothing that harms.
  • Sealed on entry — an accepted creation is fingerprinted and certificated under the same Rose Gold standard as the founding drops, and entered into the public registry, so its record can be checked by anyone.
  • Listed beside ours — the placed creation stands on this page next to the founding drops, one tap from where it can be taken home, and the world decides.

No account to open, no coin to buy, no tape to cut — a creation, a review, a seal, a place. That is the whole road.
