The Nag Hammadi Library — Gnostic Society Library

Thirteen ancient codices, over fifty texts, discovered in Upper Egypt in 1945 — including the Gospel of Thomas, the Gospel of Philip and the Gospel of Truth. The complete collection, indexed by text and by codex, with multiple translations.
